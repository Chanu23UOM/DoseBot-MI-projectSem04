The Solution

DoseBot: Five Pillars of Precision

A community kiosk that merges LabVIEW control, computer vision, and cloud connectivity into one seamless dispensing experience.

LabVIEW / DAQ Control

NI DAQ card drives servo motors with deterministic timing, ensuring accurate single-pill dispensing on every cycle.

Computer Vision Verification

Python + OpenCV webcam pipeline verifies pill color, shape, and count in real-time, logging rogue pills instantly.

Environmental Monitoring

LM35 temperature and humidity sensors keep the medicine storage zone within safe parameters, triggering alerts when thresholds are breached.

Strain Gauge Detection

Custom Wheatstone bridge strain gauge confirms correct bottle placement before any dispense cycle begins — zero false triggers.

Sinhala Voice Chatbot

AWS Bedrock + Lex powered voice assistant supports patients in Sinhala, enabling doctor consultations directly at the kiosk.
